There are four seasons in a year: spring, summer, autumn, and winter. Spring is a time when flowers bloom, trees grow new leaves, and the weather becomes warmer. Summer is usually the hottest season, with longer days that are perfect for swimming, travelling, picnics, and other outdoor activities. Autumn, also known as fall, brings cooler weather, and in many countries, leaves change colour to red, orange, and yellow before falling from the trees. Winter is the coldest season in many parts of the world, and some places experience snow and freezing temperatures. People enjoy different activities during each season, from admiring spring flowers to enjoying summer holidays, watching autumn leaves, and drinking hot chocolate during winter.
